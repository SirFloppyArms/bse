Meet Our Team

Experienced Professionals Focused on Practical Engineering Solutions

Brad S. Ellis, MASc, P.Eng., IWE

President & Lead Welding Engineer

Brad has worked in welding engineering, metallurgy, fabrication, and structural design since 2004.

He earned a BASc in Mechanical Engineering and a Master of Applied Science in Welding Engineering from the University of Waterloo.

Prior to founding BSE Welding Engineering Limited in 2010, Brad worked at New Flyer Industries, ultimately serving as Manager of Structures Engineering.

His consulting work has supported organizations throughout North America, China, and India, with a focus on practical engineering solutions, failure analysis, welding procedure development, and certification support.

Brian Miller, B.Sc. Engineering

Design, Drafting & CWB Weld Supervisor

Brian joined BSE Welding Engineering Limited in 2017 and brings more than a decade of experience in welding, fabrication, supervision, drafting, and manufacturing operations.

He has assisted numerous welders in obtaining CWB qualifications and has extensive experience helping candidates prepare for CWB Weld Supervisor examinations.

Through his expertise with SolidWorks and welded fabrication design, Brian develops practical solutions for manufacturing and production environments.
