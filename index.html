Meet Our Team

Experienced Professionals Focused on Practical Engineering Solutions

Brad S. Ellis, MASc, P.Eng., IWE

President & Lead Welding Engineer

Brad has worked in the field of welding engineering and metallurgy since 2004. A graduate of the University of Waterloo (BASc – Mechanical Engineering 2002, MASc – Welding Engineering 2004), he began at New Flyer Industries as their Weld Shop Manufacturing Engineer and progressed to become the Manager of Structures Engineering.

In 2010 he incorporated BSE Welding Engineering Limited and moved to full time consulting engineering in 2011, with New Flyer Industries becoming a valued retained client. His work has taken him to China, India and across North America. He has particular interest in failure analysis, purpose-built solutions and helping clients of all sizes succeed.

Outside of his professional work, Brad enjoys volunteering with his family including community soccer, curling and singing, splitting wood and preparing meals for a crowd.

Brian Miller, B.Sc. Engineering

Design, Drafting & CWB Weld Supervisor

Brian Miller is a University of Manitoba graduate with over 20 years of experience in the Engineering field.

Prior to joining BSE Welding Engineering in 2017, Brian spent 10 years in metals manufacturing and fabrication serving the mining, agriculture, and construction equipment industries. Brian has also participated in the management of several major infrastructure projects. As a Welding Supervisor, Brian has helped many welders achieve their CSA welder qualifications.

Brian brings his years of experience in welding, welding automation, design for manufacturing, and computer aided design and enjoys developing and implementing weld process improvement with our clients. Outside of work, Brian enjoys hockey and baseball with his kids, family camping trips in Manitoba's beautiful provincial parks, and quiet afternoons with his dog Marty.
